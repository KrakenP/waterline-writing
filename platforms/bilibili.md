# Bilibili adapter

## Primary job
Understanding, durable brand trust, and deeper causal explanation.

## Default unit
6–20 minutes depending on research depth.

## Packaging
- Hook: event/puzzle + why it matters.
- Evidence: causal chain, charts, counterthesis, and what remains unresolved.
- Structure: chapters with one question per section.
- Visuals: chart plan, diagram plan, B-roll, and screen text.
- CTA: related episode, full research, or follow Waterline for updates.

## Native output
- 5 title options.
- cover-text options.
- cold open.
- chapter outline.
- full script or detailed script skeleton.
- visual plan.
- source trace.
- CTA and compliance flags.

## Do not
- turn length into repetition.
- dramatize unsupported scenes.
- hide counterevidence to maintain narrative momentum.
