# Xiaohongshu adapter

## Primary job
Trust, saves, discovery, and repeat following.

## Default unit
- 500–1,200 Chinese characters, or
- 6–10 cards when card format is more suitable.

## Packaging
- Hook: first-person discovery, counterintuitive result, or a concrete puzzle. First-person claims must be grounded.
- Pacing: fast entry, short paragraphs, one idea per block/card.
- Evidence: 3–5 decisive facts plus one useful chart/table when available.
- Visuals: cover text + card sequence; each card should have one job.
- CTA: save/follow, invite counterevidence, or point to a deeper Waterline brief.

## Native output
- 7 ranked title options.
- 3 cover-text options.
- first two lines as a separate hook.
- platform-native post.
- optional 6–10 card plan.
- visual brief.
- tags only when useful and non-spammy.
- CTA and compliance flags.

## Do not
- fake personal experience, trades, meetings, or emotions.
- force emoji density or internet slang.
- reduce a complex conclusion to an absolute claim for engagement.
