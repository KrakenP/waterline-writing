# Xueqiu adapter

## Primary job
Investment-research trust and intelligent discussion.

## Default unit
800–2,500 Chinese characters.

## Packaging
- Hook: thesis, valuation/research implication, or a specific update trigger.
- Evidence: facts, mechanism, risks, invalidation conditions, and key dates.
- Pacing: compact and analytical; avoid excessive scene-setting.
- CTA: follow research updates or invite counterevidence; no unlicensed paid call-to-action tied to a specific-security recommendation.

## Native output
- 5 ranked title options.
- concise thesis hook.
- platform-native post.
- risks / invalidation block where relevant.
- source trace.
- CTA and compliance flags.

## Do not
- turn a company-quality view into a buy/sell instruction.
- hide downside evidence.
- use guaranteed-return or guru language.
