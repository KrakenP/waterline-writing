# WeChat Official Accounts adapter

## Primary job
Canonical free research and brand authority.

## Default unit
- 1,800–5,000+ Chinese characters when the research warrants it.
- Long form is allowed; do not pad thin research to hit length.

## Packaging
- Hook: thesis + why-now within the opening screen.
- Structure: readable causal chain with explicit evidence and counterthesis.
- Evidence: highest density among supported social platforms; retain source/date notes for material numbers.
- Visuals: charts/tables only when they prove or materially clarify a point.
- CTA: follow Waterline, continue to related research, or research subscription/website when approved.

## Native output
- 5 ranked title options.
- 1 recommended title.
- opening hook.
- full article.
- optional section headings.
- visual brief.
- source notes.
- CTA and compliance flags.

## Do not
- write like a broker sales note.
- use a sensational title that is stronger than the thesis.
- remove uncertainty merely to make the article feel conclusive.
