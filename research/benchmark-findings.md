# 2026 China finance creator benchmark — operating findings

## Executive synthesis

The 120-account sample supports three distinct distribution engines that can share one Waterline research base:

1. **Authority engine — “this institution has done the work.”** Best fit: WeChat, Xueqiu, selected Bilibili long-form. Converts depth into trust and ultimately into research subscriptions.
2. **Story engine — “I finally understand why this is happening.”** Best fit: Bilibili, Douyin, video accounts. Converts research into reach.
3. **Researcher voice — “I want to know how this researcher is thinking.”** Best fit: Xiaohongshu, Weibo, Xueqiu, plus short video. Converts repeated process visibility into relationship and return visits.

These are not three tone presets. They differ in **topic entry, claim order, evidence density, title grammar, visual packaging, update cadence and CTA**.

## Platform patterns

### Douyin
The March 2026 Feigua finance monthly ranking exposes 20 head accounts ranging from tens of万 to 2000万+ followers. The recurring packaging pattern is a fast, high-stakes question, a strong point of view, and a topical/event hook. Waterline should compress one piece into **one conflict + one mechanism**, rather than merely cutting a long report shorter.

### Xiaohongshu
The July 3, 2026 growth list is rich in process-oriented account names: “理财记”, “养基碎碎念”, “玩量化”, “Trader”, “养基日记”. Finance can grow through **first-person practice, continuity and saveable explanations**, not only institutional authority.

### Bilibili
Current 2026 examples show three useful patterns: 小Lin turns macro events into explanatory stories; 半佛 turns business cases into high-personality questions; 饭统戴老板 increasingly starts from everyday commercial puzzles while retaining research depth. Long-form depth works when **the entry point is a story or puzzle**.

### WeChat public accounts
Newrank’s 2026-01-04 wealth list mixes personal commentators, news products and research products. WeChat supports a stronger reading contract: readers tolerate density when the article offers a coherent worldview or reusable research. Use it as Waterline’s **canonical free long-form layer**.

### Xueqiu
Public creator rankings are noisier and often include unverifiable performance claims, so this benchmark uses them qualitatively. The useful mechanism is **method, process continuity, company work, risks and falsifiable updates**, not creator P&L claims.

### Weibo
The useful mechanism is speed plus recognisable judgment. A recurring short-form market inference can build habit. The unit of value is often a **sharp timely inference**, not a finished essay.

## Cross-platform rules

- Copy **mechanisms**, not creators.
- Start with a human question, contradiction, surprising observation, or falsifiable claim.
- One piece answers one central question.
- Keep the same claim state across outputs: Fact / Inference / Hypothesis / Unknown.
- Repackage one research package multiple ways; do not force one artifact onto every platform.
- CTA should move users deeper into Waterline’s research funnel, not into a stock-tip funnel.

## Do not copy

- Empty certainty (“必涨”, “稳赚”, “最后机会”).
- Fabricated personal experiences, positions or returns.
- Invented scenes or quotes.
- Fake urgency and target-price bait.
- A living creator’s distinctive catchphrases, voice or visual identity.
- Paid securities analysis / prediction / recommendation that crosses regulatory boundaries without the required qualification and review.
