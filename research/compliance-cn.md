# China finance-content guardrails

Editorial safety layer, not legal advice.

## Hard defaults

Unless an appropriately qualified and separately reviewed workflow explicitly authorizes otherwise, do not:
- sell or gate specific-security buy/sell/hold recommendations;
- provide paid target prices, entry/exit points, “one-to-one” stock diagnosis, or personalised securities advice;
- imply guaranteed returns, “稳赚”, “保本”, “无收益不收费”, or similar certainty;
- use a free post as bait for a paid group whose substance is specific-security recommendations;
- fabricate positions, returns, backtests, client outcomes, access, inside information, meetings or sources;
- present AI/quant tooling as a guaranteed stock-picking system.

## Prefer

- industry/theme research;
- company business-model and financial analysis;
- historical case studies;
- evidence-based scenarios;
- explicit uncertainty and falsification conditions;
- education about methods/risks;
- “what would change the thesis” monitoring.

## Mandatory escalation

Flag `COMPLIANCE_REVIEW_REQUIRED` if the draft includes:
- a specific listed security + explicit action verb;
- target price or exact trading level;
- personalised suitability language;
- paid access tied to a forecast/recommendation;
- claim of non-public/inside information;
- promised or implied return;
- sponsored financial-product placement.
